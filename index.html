05 — About

I've been on every side of this problem

I started with a theatre degree, which is a strange way into skincare until you've watched someone try to explain peptides to a room of forty retail associates at 8am.

I got my esthetician license in 2010 and spent years with my hands on real skin, in spas and treatment rooms, learning what people actually ask when they're worried about their face. Then I moved inside brands. Head of Global Education at Tata Harper for six and a half years, at Biossance for seven and a half. I built the curriculum, trained the field teams, and wrote the language that retail associates used at Sephora, Ulta, Credo, Nordstrom, Saks, Bluemercury, Neiman Marcus, and Space NK.

That job was translation. Chemists and NPD teams on one side, a customer standing in a store with a specific problem on the other. My job was to get one to the other without losing what was true.

Now I do it on camera myself, instead of briefing someone else to do it.

I'm also a working parent, which is less a credential than a filter. I'm not going to recommend a routine nobody has time for, and I notice when a product actually makes a day easier. It shapes what I choose to feature more than it shapes how I talk.
